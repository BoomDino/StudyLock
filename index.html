A small reward, on your terms.

Build a routine that fits your day. StudyLock keeps the rules simple and lets you decide when to spend your earned time.

◎

Your goal, your schedule

Choose a daily minimum and set the hours when you can submit a study report.

⌁

A reward that scales

Enter your actual study time. Your access grows with your progress, up to 30 minutes.

Ⅱ

Save it for later

Pause your reward whenever you like. The timer stops and Crunchyroll locks until you resume.

Simple by design

Your break starts when you’re ready.

No fixed study-hour presets. No need to use your reward right away. Report your effort, then pause or resume access to fit your evening.

1
Set your study goal

Choose your minimum daily hours and reporting window.

2
Report your time

Enter a duration such as 02:45 or 02:45:30 during your window.

3
Use your earned time

Access is calculated from your goal. Pause it and come back later.

More study, more break

Reward grows smoothly with your progress toward your personal minimum.

✓

Your data stays yours

StudyLock stores your settings, report, and timer locally in your browser. It doesn’t send your study data to a server. This version currently applies its access rules to Crunchyroll.
